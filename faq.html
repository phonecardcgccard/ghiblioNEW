Frequently Asked Questions

How does Ghiblio work?

Ghiblio uses advanced AI models to generate artwork based on your text descriptions or images. Just enter your idea or upload a picture, and let AI do the magic!

Is Ghiblio free to use?

Yes, currently all features are free to try. We may introduce premium features in the future.

Can I use the artworks commercially?

Artworks generated by Ghiblio are free for personal use. For commercial use or redistribution, please contact us for details and permissions.

How can I contribute or provide feedback?

We welcome feedback and contributions! Please open an issue on our GitHub repository or email us at contact@ghiblio.xyz.
